Questions

What does it cost?

No launch price. You pay ponsfamily.com's creation cost and network fees. Creator fees are split on chain: 25% to you, 25% buys and burns $WINAGENT, 50% to WinAgent for marketing and the team.

Who owns the token?

You do. It is created from your wallet and you are the creator on ponsfamily.com.

Is it safe?

You sign every transaction in your own wallet. WinAgent never sees your keys and never asks for a seed phrase.

Why the X profile and not a post?

First tokens that linked one X post reached 400 SOL 1.49% of the time; those that linked the account 2.8%.

I launched tokens before. Does the setup help?

Much less in our data: the same full setup from wallets with earlier launches reached 400 SOL 0.33% of the time.

Will my token go up?

Nobody can promise that. WinAgent launches the strongest setup it knows; the market decides the rest.
